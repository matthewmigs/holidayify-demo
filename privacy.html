Privacy Policy — Holidayify

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Holidayify ("the app") is a Shopify app that automatically themes a merchant's storefront for holidays and reverts those changes automatically once each holiday ends. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and how to contact us with questions.

What we collect

Holidayify collects and stores shop-level configuration data needed to operate the app:

  • Your shop's domain (e.g. yourstore.myshopify.com)
  • Your selected plan (Free, Starter, Growth, or Pro)
  • Which holiday packs you've enabled
  • Your chosen buffer and revert-time scheduling settings

If you use banner sales tracking, Holidayify also stores banner view and click totals for each holiday, and limited order data (the order's ID and number, order total and currency, discount code used, and holiday) for orders that came from the banner. See "Holiday banner sales tracking" below.

Holidayify does not collect or store customers' names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, or payment information. For banner sales tracking, the app reads new orders as they're placed to find the ones that came from the banner. It doesn't read customer records or past order history, and it has no access to checkout or payment details.

How we use this data

The shop-level settings listed above are used solely to determine which holiday theme (if any) should be applied to your storefront at a given time, and to enforce the holiday limits of your chosen plan. Banner view and click totals and the limited order data described above are used only to show you how your holiday banner performs inside Holidayify. This data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Where data is stored

Shop settings are stored in a database operated by Holidayify's hosting provider and are retained only for as long as the app remains installed on your store. When you uninstall Holidayify, your shop's settings are deleted, consistent with Shopify's mandatory compliance webhook requirements (customer data request, customer redact, and shop redact).

Holiday banner sales tracking

Holidayify can show merchants how well their holiday banner works: how often it was seen, how often its button was clicked, and which orders came from it. To do this:

  • Views and clicks are counted as totals for each holiday. We don't record who viewed or clicked, and we don't use cookies or third-party tracking scripts for this.
  • Marking the cart: when a shopper clicks the banner's button or its Copy-code button, and only if they've allowed analytics in the store's cookie/consent settings, we add a hidden marker to their cart naming the holiday (for example "bfcm-2026"). Shoppers don't see it at checkout. It's saved on the order, where the merchant can see it.
  • Order data: we read new orders from the merchant's store to find ones with that marker or with the banner's discount code. From those orders we store only the order's ID and number, the order total and currency, the discount code used, and the holiday. We never collect or store customers' names, email addresses, phone numbers or addresses.
  • Purpose: we use this information only to show the merchant their banner results inside Holidayify. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or combine it with data from other stores.
  • How long we keep it: order records are kept for 13 months, after which only totals remain. If a merchant uninstalls Holidayify, all of their data is deleted. If a shopper asks a store to delete their data, we delete any of their orders we hold.
  • Security: data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with our hosting provider, Railway, which encrypts data at rest.

Billing data

Paid plan subscriptions are processed entirely through Shopify's own Billing API. Holidayify never receives, stores, or has access to your payment card details — that information is handled directly by Shopify.

Cookies and tracking

Holidayify doesn't set its own cookies or use third-party analytics scripts. If a merchant uses banner sales tracking, we count banner views and clicks and, with the shopper's analytics consent, add a hidden marker to the cart, as described in "Holiday banner sales tracking" above.

Your rights

You can request details of what data is stored for your shop, or request deletion of that data, at any time by contacting us at the email below. Uninstalling the app also automatically deletes your shop's stored settings.
